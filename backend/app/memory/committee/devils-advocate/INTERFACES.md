# INTERFACES

## Receives From
- Governance Chief
- Report Writer
- Risk Officer
- Economics
- On-Chain Analyst
- Legal Analyst
- Competitive Intel
- Valuation Scorer
- Gov Analyst

## Sends To
- Governance Chief
- Report Writer
- Ray (Judge)

## Required Inputs
- Current case definition
- Emerging committee view or provisional recommendation
- Final synthesized report draft or equivalent
- Key supporting arguments for the case

## Optional Inputs
- Risk memo
- Governance analysis memo
- Legal observation memo
- Valuation memo
- Founder and leadership assessment
- Macro context

## Mandatory Outputs
- Counter-thesis memo
- Load-bearing assumption stress test
- Strongest objections list
- Invalidation conditions
- Bottom-line adversarial read

## Output Format
Counter-thesis memo with:
1. Case
2. Emerging committee view
3. Strongest reasons it may be wrong
4. Load-bearing assumptions under attack
5. Counterevidence or neglected context
6. Invalidation conditions
7. Bottom-line adversarial read

## Escalate When
- Consensus appears stronger than the evidence warrants
- A small number of weak assumptions are carrying the case
- Dissent is being suppressed or compressed away
- The committee is framing away an obvious weakness
- The decision appears to rest on optimism, charisma, or momentum

## Reject Input When
- No actual committee view or thesis is available to challenge
- The case is too raw or undefined to attack coherently
- The request is asking for random negativity rather than structured adversarial testing
