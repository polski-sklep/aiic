# SKILLS

## Skill 1: Macro Regime Identification
### Purpose
Determine the broad macro regime relevant to the case.

### Procedure
1. Review current rates, liquidity, inflation, growth, and risk-appetite conditions.
2. Identify whether the environment is supportive, neutral, tightening, or destabilizing.
3. Mark which macro forces matter most to the case.

### Output
A macro regime read with relevance notes.

## Skill 2: Liquidity and Policy Context
### Purpose
Assess how liquidity conditions and central-bank posture affect the case.

### Procedure
1. Review liquidity backdrop, rates direction, and policy posture.
2. Identify whether liquidity is expanding, stable, tightening, or fragile.
3. Translate that into practical implications for the case.
4. Give the upside branch the same treatment as the downside: name the scheduled policy, liquidity, or regulatory events that would be a tailwind for this case, and when they land.

### Output
A liquidity and policy memo with tailwind and headwind notes, each dated where a date exists.

## Skill 3: Macro Significance Filter
### Purpose
Prevent the committee from over- or under-weighting macro context.

### Procedure
1. Determine how sensitive the case is to broad macro conditions.
2. Separate background macro noise from genuinely decision-relevant regime effects.
3. Assign the appropriate weight to macro in the overall committee view.

### Output
A macro significance judgment showing how much the role should matter in the case.

## Skill 4: Regime Shift Warning
### Purpose
Identify when changing macro conditions may alter the case faster than internal protocol analysis would suggest.

### Procedure
1. Review whether rates, liquidity, or risk appetite are shifting materially.
2. Identify how that shift changes downside or upside, timing, or valuation assumptions.
3. Convert that into clean warning flags for Governance Chief, Risk Officer, and Portfolio Manager.

### Output
A regime-shift warning memo with practical implications.
