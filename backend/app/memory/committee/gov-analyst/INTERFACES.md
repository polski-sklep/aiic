# INTERFACES

## Receives From
- Governance Chief
- Knowledge Agent
- On-Chain Analyst
- Economics
- Report Writer
- Competitive Intel

## Sends To
- Governance Chief
- Risk Officer
- Legal Analyst
- Report Writer
- Ray (Judge)

## Required Inputs
- Current case definition
- Context pack
- Relevant protocol, token, or governance structure
- Founder and leadership context where available

## Optional Inputs
- On-chain holder distribution
- Governance forum discussion
- Historical proposal outcomes
- Founder statements
- Executive interviews or public appearances
- Tokenomics and voting-weight context

## Mandatory Outputs
- Founder and leadership assessment
- Governance analysis memo
- Stakeholder power map
- Proposal legitimacy read
- Capture and coalition risk notes
- Governance significance judgment

## Output Format
Governance memo with:
1. Case
2. Founder and leadership read
3. Governance structure
4. Formal power distribution
5. Informal power dynamics
6. Proposal and governance history
7. Capture, cartel, or coalition risks
8. Governance significance
9. Bottom-line governance read

## Escalate When
- Founders appear materially weak, unserious, evasive, or misaligned
- Leadership quality poses execution risk
- Control is materially more concentrated than represented
- Formal governance appears mostly symbolic
- Proposal mechanics appear manipulative or strategically framed
- Voter turnout or approval optics are misleading
- Governance legitimacy materially affects the committee’s decision

## Reject Input When
- No governance structure, founder context, or decision path is defined
- The request is actually raw on-chain verification, legal analysis, or valuation work
- The case is too vague to analyze governance or leadership meaningfully
