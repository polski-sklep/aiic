# SKILLS

## Skill 1: Founder and Leadership Assessment
### Purpose
Determine whether the founders and executive team are capable, serious, aligned, and fit to lead the protocol.

### Procedure
1. Review founder history, public behavior, communication style, execution record, and consistency over time.
2. Assess competence, seriousness, self-awareness, discipline, integrity, and operational credibility.
3. Identify whether the leadership team increases confidence, creates concern, or poses existential risk.

### Output
A founder and leadership memo with strengths, weaknesses, red flags, and overall quality judgment.

## Skill 2: Stakeholder Power Mapping
### Purpose
Identify who actually influences governance outcomes and how much power they hold.

### Procedure
1. Identify major token holders, founders, core team members, foundations, delegates, and aligned blocs.
2. Separate formal voting weight from informal influence.
3. Map which actors can block, shape, or dominate outcomes.

### Output
A stakeholder power map with influence notes and concentration warnings.

## Skill 3: Proposal and Governance History Analysis
### Purpose
Assess whether governance has functioned credibly in practice rather than only in theory.

### Procedure
1. Review proposal structure, framing, timing, thresholds, turnout, and execution path.
2. Examine past governance proposals, how they played out, and how they were received by stakeholders.
3. Mark whether governance has been legitimate, cosmetic, manipulated, ineffective, or operationally meaningful.

### Output
A governance history memo with legitimacy, participation, and outcome notes.

## Skill 4: Capture and Coalition Risk Assessment
### Purpose
Determine whether governance can be bent, dominated, cartelized, or gamed by concentrated actors.

### Procedure
1. Review holder concentration, delegate structure, turnout patterns, founder influence, and informal power.
2. Identify whether coalitions, blocs, or cartels have formed or are likely to form.
3. Flag where capture risk is structural, temporary, overstated, or unavoidable.

### Output
A capture and coalition-risk memo with severity and confidence labels.

## Skill 5: Governance Significance Read
### Purpose
Translate founder and governance analysis into a practical committee judgment.

### Procedure
1. Determine whether leadership quality and governance meaningfully affect value, control, legitimacy, or execution risk.
2. Separate superficial governance noise from decision-relevant control dynamics.
3. Produce a bottom-line read usable by Governance Chief, Risk Officer, Legal Analyst, and Ray.

### Output
A governance significance judgment for downstream committee use.
