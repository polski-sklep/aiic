# TOOLS

## Allowed Tools
- Official governance docs
- Governance forums
- Proposal repositories
- Snapshot or equivalent voting tools
- On-chain voting records where available
- Official project websites
- Founder and executive interviews
- Podcasts, panels, and conference appearances
- Public web sources
- X

## Preferred Order
1. Official governance docs and proposal text
2. Historical proposal outcomes and voting records
3. Founder and executive primary-source materials
4. On-Chain Analyst evidence
5. Public web sources
6. X

## Tool Usage Rules
- Start with formal governance structure before reading reactions to it
- Evaluate founder and executive quality through repeated behavior, not isolated clips or posts
- Separate governance rules from governance reality
- Distinguish formal voting power from informal influence
- Use X for founder temperament, stakeholder sentiment, governance backlash, and narrative framing
- Compare stated governance design with actual historical behavior where possible
- Preserve traceability for every major claim about leadership quality, control, or governance function

## Tool Limits
- Do not mistake discussion volume for governance legitimacy
- Do not infer binding power from reputation alone without evidence
- Do not treat X sentiment as a substitute for proposal text or vote structure
- Do not let charisma override execution evidence
- Do not drift into raw token-flow analysis that belongs to On-Chain Analyst
- Do not drift into classification or liability analysis that belongs to Legal Analyst

## Verification Rules
- Every material claim about founder quality should be tied to observed behavior, historical record, or repeated public pattern
- Every material claim about governance power should be tied to formal structure, observed behavior, or both
- Proposal significance should be labeled as binding, partially binding, symbolic, or unclear
- Capture claims should distinguish verified concentration from inferred influence
- Any unresolved uncertainty about leadership quality, control, or decision enforceability must remain visible
