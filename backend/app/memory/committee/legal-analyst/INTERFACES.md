# INTERFACES

## Receives From
- Governance Chief
- Knowledge Agent
- On-Chain Analyst
- Economics
- Gov Analyst
- Report Writer

## Sends To
- Risk Officer
- Governance Chief
- Report Writer

## Required Inputs
- Current case definition
- Context pack
- Relevant entity and jurisdiction information
- Token, protocol, proposal, or transaction structure
- Relevant disclosures, terms, or documentation

## Optional Inputs
- On-chain evidence
- Governance proposal text
- Investor announcements
- Founder statements
- Historical comparable cases

## Mandatory Outputs
- Legal and structural observation memo
- Jurisdictional posture summary
- Documentation and disclosure weakness list
- Notable compliance or classification issues where relevant
- Risk flags for Risk Officer

## Output Format
Legal observation memo with:
1. Case
2. Jurisdictional setup
3. Documentation and disclosure quality
4. Structural or regulatory observations
5. Ordinary vs concerning issues
6. Major uncertainties
7. Risk flags for Risk Officer
8. Bottom-line legal/structural read

## Escalate When
- The structure looks materially deceptive or intentionally obscured
- Documentation is incomplete, misleading, or internally inconsistent in a decision-relevant way
- A major jurisdiction creates meaningful exposure
- Governance or control structure creates legitimacy or liability concerns
- Legal ambiguity materially changes the risk profile

## Reject Input When
- No relevant structure, entity, or jurisdiction can be identified
- The request is actually economic, portfolio, or market analysis rather than legal observation
- The facts are too incomplete to produce even a directional structural read
