# SKILLS

## Skill 1: Jurisdiction and Structure Read
### Purpose
Identify how the entity or protocol is structured and whether that structure looks ordinary, aggressive, sloppy, or deceptive.

### Procedure
1. Identify the relevant entities, jurisdictions, and operating footprint.
2. Note where incorporation, token issuance, treasury control, or governance structure may matter.
3. Distinguish standard crypto setup from unusual or concerning structural choices.

### Output
A jurisdiction and structure memo with practical observations.

## Skill 2: Documentation and Disclosure Review
### Purpose
Assess whether the project’s formal materials are coherent enough to support informed judgment.

### Procedure
1. Review terms, disclosures, governance texts, tokenomics claims, and public-facing legal language.
2. Identify omissions, contradictions, vague claims, and sloppy drafting.
3. Mark which weaknesses are routine and which materially increase risk.

### Output
A documentation weakness list with severity notes.

## Skill 3: Regulatory and Classification Observation
### Purpose
Identify obvious regulatory or classification issues without pretending to deliver formal legal conclusions.

### Procedure
1. Review the asset, structure, user-facing claims, and governance setup.
2. Note plausible regulatory sensitivities or classification questions.
3. Separate light observations from issues that materially affect the risk profile.

### Output
A regulatory observation memo with confidence labels.

## Skill 4: Risk Flag Escalation
### Purpose
Translate legal and structural observations into practical warnings for the Risk Officer and Governance Chief.

### Procedure
1. Identify which legal or structural issues are merely common market practice.
2. Isolate the issues that could realistically affect enforcement, credibility, market trust, or decision integrity.
3. Convert those into clean risk flags with severity.

### Output
A risk flag memo for downstream use by Risk Officer and Governance Chief.
