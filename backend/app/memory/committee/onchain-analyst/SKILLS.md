# SKILLS

## Skill 1: Wallet Attribution and Mapping
### Purpose
Identify the most relevant wallets, contracts, and entities connected to the case and separate verified attribution from inference.

### Procedure
1. Identify known official wallets, treasury addresses, vesting contracts, staking contracts, and major holder addresses.
2. Map relationships between addresses, contracts, and counterparties where evidence exists.
3. Label each attribution as verified, inferred, unknown, or disputed.

### Output
A wallet and entity map with confidence labels.

## Skill 2: Flow Analysis
### Purpose
Understand how tokens or assets are actually moving across wallets, exchanges, contracts, and treasury systems.

### Procedure
1. Trace meaningful inflows, outflows, and transfers across relevant addresses.
2. Separate routine operational movement from suspicious, strategic, or unusual movement.
3. Highlight exchange deposits, treasury transfers, insider-looking flows, and large concentration shifts.

### Output
A flow analysis showing what moved, where, when, and why it may matter.

## Skill 3: Concentration and Supply Verification
### Purpose
Test whether the observed holder structure and circulating reality match public claims.

### Procedure
1. Review top holders, vesting contracts, treasury balances, and liquid versus locked supply.
2. Compare observable on-chain supply structure to public tokenomics claims.
3. Flag hidden concentration, unclear float, or supply discrepancies.

### Output
A concentration and supply verification memo with contradictions and unresolved gaps.

## Skill 4: Behavioral Pattern Detection
### Purpose
Spot patterns in on-chain activity that may indicate insider behavior, market preparation, treasury stress, or narrative mismatch.

### Procedure
1. Review time-series behavior around key events such as proposals, unlocks, listings, or announcements.
2. Identify repeated patterns in movement, clustering, or exchange interaction.
3. Mark which patterns are observable facts and which interpretations remain tentative.

### Output
A behavioral pattern memo with red flags, alternative explanations, and confidence levels.
