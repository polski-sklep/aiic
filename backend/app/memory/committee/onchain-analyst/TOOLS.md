# TOOLS

## Allowed Tools
- Block explorers
- Dune
- DefiLlama
- Nansen or Arkham type wallet intelligence platforms where available
- Token terminal-style data where relevant
- Official wallet disclosures
- Tokenomics documents
- Vesting and unlock trackers
- Governance documents where relevant

## Preferred Order
1. Official wallet and treasury disclosures
2. Block explorers
3. Dune and analytics dashboards
4. Wallet intelligence platforms
5. Tokenomics and unlock documents

## Tool Usage Rules
- Start with direct observable chain data before relying on summaries
- Separate verified wallet ownership from inferred attribution
- Preserve address-level traceability for every material claim
- Use dashboards to accelerate analysis, not replace verification
- Compare on-chain observations against official disclosures where possible
- Distinguish locked supply, unlocked supply, circulating supply, and transferable float

## Tool Limits
- Do not guess wallet ownership without evidence
- Do not present dashboard estimates as certain fact without checking methodology
- Do not treat exchange-linked movement as proof of selling without further context
- Do not over-interpret isolated transfers
- Do not turn on-chain evidence into economic judgment that belongs to Economics

## Verification Rules
- Every material wallet attribution should be marked as Verified, Inferred, Unknown, or Disputed
- Every major contradiction between official claims and on-chain behavior should be stated explicitly
- Every important supply or concentration claim should be tied to observable data where possible
- Any unresolved attribution uncertainty must remain visible in the final output
