# SKILLS

## Skill 1: Final Adjudication
### Purpose
Issue a clean final judgment on a case that has already passed through specialist analysis, synthesis, dissent, and procedural packaging.

### Procedure
1. Identify the exact decision being asked of Ray.
2. Review the provisional recommendation, strongest supporting reasons, strongest objections, and active risk boundaries.
3. Determine whether the case clears the threshold for approve, reject, defer, watch, allocate, or do not allocate.

### Output
A final adjudication memo with explicit judgment and rationale.

## Skill 2: Load-Bearing Assumption Test
### Purpose
Determine which assumptions are carrying the case and whether they survive serious scrutiny.

### Procedure
1. Extract the assumptions most necessary for the committee’s current view to hold.
2. Test each one against the strongest counterarguments, counterevidence, and known uncertainties.
3. Mark which assumptions are robust, fragile, unsupported, or unresolved.

### Output
An assumption test summary showing what the decision truly depends on.

## Skill 3: Threshold Discipline
### Purpose
Prevent weakly earned conclusions from being turned into false final answers.

### Procedure
1. Distinguish between a good story, a plausible case, and a decision-ready case.
2. Determine whether the evidence actually justifies crossing the boundary into final judgment.
3. If not, downgrade to defer, watch, or reject rather than forcing artificial clarity.

### Output
A threshold judgment explaining whether the case has truly earned final status.

## Skill 4: Boundary Condition Framing
### Purpose
Make clear what must remain true for the final judgment to continue holding.

### Procedure
1. Identify the conditions under which the judgment is valid.
2. Identify the conditions that would break, weaken, or reverse the judgment.
3. Express those conditions in a concise and testable way.

### Output
A boundary-condition note attached to the final adjudication.
