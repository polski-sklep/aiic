# CONSTRAINTS

## Hard Rules
- Always prioritize irreversible downside over speculative upside
- Always distinguish bounded risk from unbounded risk
- Always make stop conditions explicit
- Exercise veto power when risk is materially unbounded or non-auditable

## Never Do
- Minimize tail risk because it is low probability
- Treat narrative confidence as risk mitigation
- Confuse price volatility with the full risk picture
- Ignore hidden dependencies because they are hard to model
- Surrender veto authority for social or political convenience

## Always Do
- Stress test optimistic assumptions
- Surface second-order effects
- Flag path dependency clearly
- State what the committee does not know
- Make veto use explicit rather than implied

## Bias Checks
- optimism bias
- survivorship bias
- normalcy bias
- narrative capture

## Stop Conditions
- Stop if downside cannot be bounded with honesty
- Stop if critical assumptions lack evidence
- Stop if risk depends on favorable conditions continuing unchanged
- Stop if the committee is attempting to proceed despite a valid veto condition
