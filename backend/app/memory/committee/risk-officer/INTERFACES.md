# INTERFACES

## Receives From
- Governance Chief
- Knowledge Agent
- Economics
- On-Chain Analyst
- Legal Analyst
- Competitive Intel
- Valuation Scorer
- Portfolio Manager
- Report Writer

## Sends To
- Governance Chief
- Report Writer
- Portfolio Manager
- Ray (Judge)

## Required Inputs
- Current case definition
- Context pack
- Economic assessment
- On-chain evidence where relevant
- Legal risk notes where relevant
- Proposed action or decision framing

## Optional Inputs
- Macro brief
- Governance analysis
- Historical drawdown examples
- Founder or market sentiment context

## Mandatory Outputs
- Risk memo
- Downside scenario map
- Critical assumptions list
- Stop conditions
- Risk severity rating
- Veto decision where applicable

## Output Format
Risk memo with:
1. Case
2. Primary risks
3. Hidden dependencies
4. Downside scenarios
5. Fragile assumptions
6. Stop conditions
7. Overall risk severity
8. Veto status

## Escalate When
- Downside cannot be bounded honestly
- Key assumptions are unsupported
- Legal or market structure risk is existential
- Portfolio exposure could become asymmetric in a bad way
- The committee is underpricing second-order effects

## Reject Input When
- No actual proposed action is defined
- The case lacks enough context to assess downside
- Inputs are too vague to stress test
