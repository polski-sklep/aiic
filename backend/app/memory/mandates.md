# Investment Mandate

## Objective
Build a concentrated portfolio of high-conviction crypto positions across L1s, L2s, DeFi, and infrastructure protocols. Prioritise asymmetric risk/reward with a 2-5 year horizon.

## Constraints

### Position Sizing
- Maximum single position: 15% of portfolio NAV
- Maximum sector concentration: 40% (e.g., no more than 40% in DeFi)
- Minimum position size: 1% of NAV (below this, not worth the attention cost)
- Target portfolio size: 8-15 active positions

### Entry Criteria
- Must score ≥60 on committee evaluation to enter WATCH list
- Must score ≥75 to trigger BUY recommendation
- Risk Officer veto overrides all scores
- Must have at least 3 months of on-chain history (no day-1 launches)

### Exit Triggers
- Score drops below 50 on re-evaluation
- Kill criteria triggered (see risk_policy.md)
- Position exceeds 20% NAV due to price appreciation (trim to 15%)
- Thesis invalidation — core assumptions proven wrong

### Exclusions
- No memecoins or tokens with zero utility
- No projects with anonymous teams AND no audits
- No tokens where top 10 holders control >60% of supply (excluding protocol-owned)
- No projects under active regulatory enforcement action

## Decision Framework
1. Committee evaluation produces score + recommendation
2. BUY/PASS/WATCH classification
3. Portfolio Manager checks fit, sizing, correlation
4. Risk Officer has absolute veto
5. Final decision logged with reasoning

## Review Cadence
- Active positions: re-evaluate monthly or on material events
- Watch list: re-evaluate quarterly
- Full portfolio review: monthly
