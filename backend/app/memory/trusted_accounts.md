## Red Flag Signals
When these accounts flag issues, treat as HIGH PRIORITY:
- @zachxbt exposing fraud or insider activity = immediate escalation to Risk Officer
- @samczsun flagging security concerns = immediate escalation to Risk Officer
- @PeckShieldAlert or @CertiKAlert reporting exploits = veto consideration
- @based16z or @VannaCharmer cynical analysis of a project
- Multiple trusted accounts expressing concern simultaneously = systemic risk signal

## Credibility Rules
1. Verified accounts with >10K followers discussing the specific project = high weight
2. Security researchers flagging issues = treat as near-confirmed until disproven
3. @zachxbt investigations = treat as confirmed unless project provides verifiable rebuttal
4. Fund accounts discussing positions = note potential bias (they may be talking their book)
5. High-signal shitposters consistently targeting a project = strong negative signal, investigate the underlying structural point
6. Anonymous accounts regardless of follower count = low weight unless corroborated
7. Accounts with promotional language, rocket emojis, or "100x" claims = noise, discard
8. Multiple trusted accounts saying the same thing = strong signal
9. Trusted account contradicting the bull case = must be addressed, not ignored
